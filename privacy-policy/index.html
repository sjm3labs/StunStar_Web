StunStar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-29

suseong kim ("the developer") handles the personal information of people who play the mobile game "StunStar" ("the game") as set out below.

1. Information collected

  • Advertising identifier (advertising ID) and app installation identifier (app instance ID)
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, country, IP address
  • Approximate location (country or city level) estimated from your IP address, for adverts and fraud prevention. Precise location (GPS) is not collected.
  • Gameplay records: levels, wins and losses, item purchases and adverts watched, times of use
  • Game account: an anonymous player ID and a generated name, created automatically when the game is first opened, with the weekly ranking records (adventure and online match) tied to them
  • Reward records: the last attendance reward and weekly ranking reward you received, and a time check against the server, saved in the cloud for every player whether or not an account is linked, so that a reward is not paid twice
  • If you link an account (a Unity account, signed in with Google, Apple or an email): the account's identifier, and your game progress saved in the cloud (level, currencies, items and the like). Without a link, progress is kept on your device only. The game does not store the email or name of the account you sign in with.
  • Error information: the error, the state of the device (memory, storage, screen orientation and the like) and the time, when the game meets an error or stops unexpectedly
  • Live matches:
    • Local match (same Wi-Fi): your device's address and the room name (which may include your device's name) are made known on that network.
    • Online match: the game goes through a relay server run by the developer. The server handles your IP address, your player ID (to confirm your sign-in), the room's details (room number, generated name, profile character and level, room settings and a hash of its password) and connection times, and passes the match data between the two devices.

The game does not collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

With "shake to wake" on, the device's motion sensor (accelerometer) is used only to tell when you shake the phone while stunned in a match. Its readings are used on the device alone and are neither stored nor sent.

2. Purposes

  • Providing the game: identifying players, running the weekly rankings and paying their rewards
  • Online matches: making and joining rooms, relaying matches, preventing abuse (one connection per account at a time, no joining one's own room) and managing server load
  • Preventing rewards from being paid twice: reward records and checking the time against the server
  • Account linking: keeping the progress of a linked player and letting it continue on another device
  • Advertising: showing rewarded adverts, paying their rewards, measuring their results and preventing fraud
  • Improving the game: usage statistics, difficulty tuning and fixing errors

3. What other players see

  • The weekly rankings (adventure and online match) show your generated name, your profile or played character and your score to the other players in your group.
  • In a live match the other player sees your generated name, level, character and its stats, and the games each has won.
  • The online match room list shows the room number, the host's generated name, profile character and level, whether the room has a password, and the room settings.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Game data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app.
  • Your account, records and reward records on servers are kept while the service is provided and are deleted without delay on request or when you delete your account.
  • The relay server does not store match data. To let a dropped match be resumed it keeps only the last match state for a short time (about 70 seconds). Its operating records (connection number, room number and times) are kept for up to 30 days for running the service and dealing with abuse, then deleted. They hold no player ID, and IP addresses only in part.
  • Usage statistics and error reports are deleted automatically after the period set by Google's retention policy.
  • Information that the law requires to be kept is kept for that period.
  • Deleted information is erased from the servers so that it cannot be recovered.

7. How your information is protected

  • Encryption in transit: traffic between the game and Google's and Unity's services and the online match relay server is encrypted (HTTPS, TLS). Local matches on the same Wi-Fi are not encrypted.
  • Passwords: an online match room's password reaches the server only as a hash, never as written.
  • Access control: data saved in the cloud can be read and written only after that player's sign-in is verified. The relay server's administration works only from the server itself, and the administrator accounts of the server and of each service are used by the developer alone.
  • Keeping little: the relay server's logs hold no player ID and IP addresses only in part, and are deleted when their retention period ends.

7. Medidas de segurança
